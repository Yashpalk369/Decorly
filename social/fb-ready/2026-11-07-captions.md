## 1. Neo Deco living room ideas for small apartments
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-07/pin-neo-deco-living-room.jpg
Pin title (86 chars): 10 Neo Deco Living Room Ideas for Small Apartments – 1920s Glam Decor, Budget Art Deco
Intended board (pending): Small Apartment Ideas

https://decorly.homes/neo-deco-living-room-ideas-small-apartments

✨ Neo Deco is the 1920s hotel lobby look, shrunk down for a rental living room: sunburst mirrors, jewel tones, brass and arches — all of it doable without drilling, painting or spending much. The trick is to go glamorous on the movable things and leave the walls alone.
🌿 Start with a sunburst mirror over the sofa: it is the single fastest way to get the Deco look, bounces light around a small room, and hangs from one picture hook.
🌿 Go jewel-tone on the sofa, not the walls: emerald, sapphire or ruby cushions and throws give the rich Deco palette while the walls stay landlord-white.
🌿 Use arches wherever the room will allow them: an arched mirror, arched wall art or an arched bookshelf echoes the curved Deco silhouette without any building work.

## 3. How to decorate a rental front door for Christmas

Intended board (pending): Christmas Decor

https://decorly.homes/decorate-rental-front-door-christmas-guide

🎄 A rental front door can still be the prettiest on the street — no nails, no screws, no deposit risk. The whole look hangs from over-door hooks, adhesive hooks and a bit of planning.
🌿 Hang the wreath without a single hole: an over-door wreath hanger hooks over the top of the door and holds the wreath on the outside — nothing drilled, nothing stuck.
🌿 Frame the door with a garland: drape it along the top and sides of the frame and hold it with adhesive hooks rated for outdoor use.
🌿 Add battery lights around the frame: battery fairy lights need no outlet by the door, and a timer keeps them glowing every evening without you touching them.
