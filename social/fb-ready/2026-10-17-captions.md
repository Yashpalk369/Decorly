🦃 Hosting Thanksgiving in a rental with a tiny oven? Totally doable — 7 steps built for small kitchens, borrowed chairs, and a deposit you'd like to keep.

✨ Engineer the menu around the one-oven bottleneck: cranberry sauce, pie dough, stuffing and desserts all taste better made a day or two ahead
✨ Borrow seating and serving pieces — don't buy anything you can't use again
✨ Protect the rental table with layers (runner + placemats + trivets), then style it with LED pillar candles, pinecones and kraft-paper place cards — festive for almost nothing
