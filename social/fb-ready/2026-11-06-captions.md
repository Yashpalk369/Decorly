## 1. Colourful maximalist ideas for small apartments
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-06/pin-colourful-maximalist.jpg
Pin title (96 chars): 10 Colourful Maximalist Ideas for Small Apartments – Bold Apartment Decor, Colourful Living Room
Intended board (pending): Small Apartment Ideas

https://decorly.homes/colourful-maximalist-small-apartment-ideas

🎨 Maximalism has a reputation problem in small flats: mention bold colour and layered pattern to a renter with 40 square metres and they will picture chaos. Yet the most joyful small homes right now prove the opposite — when colour is chosen with intent and every object earns its place, a small apartment can be vivid, personal and calm.
🌿 Pick a three-colour palette and repeat it everywhere: choose three colours you love — say terracotta, sage and blush — and repeat them in different proportions all through the room. Maximalism only tips into clutter when every colour in the rainbow competes at once.
🌿 Build a gallery wall with one unifying frame colour: paint every frame the same colour, or use identical slim frames throughout, and let the art be as wild as you like. The single unifying element keeps it calm.
🌿 Layer pattern on pattern, but vary the scale: pair a large-scale floral cushion with a small-scale stripe, or a bold geometric rug with a fine gingham throw. When the patterns are different sizes, the eye can tell them apart and enjoy them both.

## 2. Renter-friendly Christmas card display ideas
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-06/pin-christmas-card-display.jpg
Pin title (94 chars): 10 Renter-Friendly Christmas Card Display Ideas – No-Hole Card Displays, Small Space Christmas
Intended board (pending): Christmas Decor

https://decorly.homes/renter-friendly-christmas-card-display-ideas

💌 Christmas cards arrive in a cheerful trickle all through December — and then comes the question every renter in a small flat knows well: where do they all go? Piled on the sideboard, they slide into a heap. Blu-tacked to the wall, they leave marks and take paint with them in January.
🌿 A fairy light string with mini wooden pegs: a length of battery fairy lights strung across a wall or along the top of a bookshelf, with cards pegged to it using tiny wooden pegs, turns a pile of post into a glowing feature in one evening.
🌿 A washi tape gallery wall: washi tape sticks firmly enough to hold a card all month, peels off without taking paint, and comes in every festive pattern you can imagine. A gallery wall of cards held up with small washi tape corners looks deliberate and polished.
🌿 A tension rod across a window or alcove: it wedges between two surfaces with pressure alone — no screws, no marks — and can span a window frame, a shallow alcove or the gap between two bookshelves. Hang ribbon or twine from the rod and clip cards to it.

## 3. Starburst light fixtures under $25 for small rentals
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-06/pin-starburst-light-fixtures.jpg
Pin title (100 chars): 9 Starburst Light Fixtures Under $25 for Small Rentals – Mid-Century Lighting, Budget Ceiling Lights
Intended board (pending): Budget Decor Finds

https://decorly.homes/starburst-light-fixtures-small-rentals-under-25

✨ There is a particular kind of magic that happens when a plain rental ceiling gets a starburst light. The room does not just get brighter — it gets a focal point, a little mid-century drama, a constellation you can switch on. Starburst and sputnik fixtures are the centrepiece of the celestial lighting trend Pinterest flagged for 2026.
🌿 No hardwiring needed: every fixture in this roundup is plug-in, battery-powered or a bulb swap. Plug-in pendants hang from a single ceiling hook and run a cord to the nearest outlet; adhesive hooks work for lighter fixtures.
🌿 Match the scale to the room: for rooms under about 12 square metres, choose a mini sputnik (30–40 cm across) or a tabletop starburst lamp rather than a full-size fixture. Hang pendants at least 2 metres from the floor in walkways so nobody walks into them.
🌿 Choose warm white, around 2700K: it is the cosiest choice and flatters the brass and gold finishes most starburst fixtures use. Cool white makes the metal look harsh and the room feel clinical.

## 4. How to add a striped ceiling to a rental bedroom
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-06/pin-striped-ceiling-bedroom.jpg
Pin title (90 chars): How to Add a Striped Ceiling to a Rental Bedroom – Removable Ceiling Decor, No Paint Ideas
Intended board (pending): Small Apartment Ideas

https://decorly.homes/striped-ceiling-bedroom-rental-guide

🛏️ A striped ceiling is the single most dramatic thing you can do to a small rental bedroom — and, if you do it right, the most reversible. Pinterest Predicts 2026 flagged the striped ceiling as a breakout FunHaus trend, with searches for bold striped ceilings up 40% year on year. Stripes pull the eye upward, make a low ceiling feel taller, and turn a plain white box into a room with a point of view.
🌿 Measure your ceiling and plan the stripe layout first: measure the length and the width in two places each, because rental ceilings are rarely perfectly square. Stripes look best when they run along the longest wall of the room.
🌿 Choose the right removable material: removable peel-and-stick wallpaper sold in narrow rolls or pre-cut stripe lengths is made to come off cleanly and holds its own weight overhead. Washi tape is a cheaper, lighter alternative that works beautifully for narrow stripes.
🌿 Gather your tools and prep the surface: a sturdy step ladder, a long spirit level or laser level, a tape measure, a sharp craft knife and a metal ruler, a pencil, a smoothing tool or an old credit card, and a clean dry cloth. A second pair of hands helps enormously.

## 5. How to style a woven basket wall with trailing plants
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-06/pin-woven-basket-wall.jpg
Pin title (89 chars): How to Style a Woven Basket Wall with Trailing Plants – Boho Wall Decor, Plant Wall Ideas
Intended board (pending): Houseplants

https://decorly.homes/woven-basket-wall-trailing-plants-guide

🧺 A woven basket wall is the kind of decor that looks far more expensive than it is. A handful of seagrass, jute and rattan baskets, a few trailing plants and a single afternoon can turn a bare rental wall into the warmest spot in your home — no drilling, no paint and no landlord drama. It is also one of the standout looks of the Afrohemian trend, the Pinterest Predicts 2026 favourite.
🌿 Pick your wall and map the layout first: the best spot is one you look at every day but never quite know what to do with — the wall behind a sofa, the blank stretch above a sideboard, or a narrow hallway wall that currently holds nothing but a light switch.
🌿 Choose baskets in mixed sizes, shapes and textures: the single biggest mistake with basket walls is buying a matching set. A row of identical baskets looks like a shop display; a mix looks collected, personal and expensive. Aim for five to nine baskets for a statement wall, or three to five for a smaller space.
🌿 Pick trailing plants that cascade beautifully: golden pothos is the classic for a reason — it grows fast, tolerates low light and neglect, and its heart-shaped leaves look lovely against woven fibres.
