## 2. keep-poinsettias-alive-guide
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-25/pin-keep-poinsettias-alive.jpg
URL: https://decorly.homes/keep-poinsettias-alive-guide/

Post: https://decorly.homes/keep-poinsettias-alive-guide/

🌺 Most poinsettias die before they reach your living room — here's how yours survives.

Poinsettias aren't delicate: they're tropical shrubs that stay glorious through
Christmas given bright light, careful watering, and protection from cold and
heat blasts. The usual killers are drowning, freezing, or baking above a radiator.

Tips from the guide:
• Get it home without freezing it — insist on a sleeve, and never leave it in
  a cold car; poinsettias start suffering below about 10°C.
• Water by weight, not by calendar: lift the pot, water only when the top 2–3 cm
  of soil is dry, and never let it sit in standing water (the foil wrapper is
  packaging, not a planter).
• Keep it at a steady 15–22°C, away from cold window glass and radiators — the
  windowsill directly above a radiator is the classic killer spot.

6 steps to keep it alive (and rebloom it next year): https://decorly.homes/keep-poinsettias-alive-guide/

🧶 A $20 throw is the renter's secret weapon — no paint, no new sofa, whole room changed.

You can't paint the walls or change the landlord's sofa, but a throw adds colour,
texture and warmth in one move — and it moves with you to the next flat. Every
pick here is under $25 and machine-washable.

Tips from the guide:
• Chunky cable-knit ($20–25): the thick knit traps air, which is what actually
  keeps you warm — cream or oatmeal goes with everything.
• Sherpa-lined fleece ($15–22): the warmest blanket per dollar; sherpa side
  against you, fleece side out for a clean look.
• Waffle-weave cotton ($15–20): the three-season option — breathable when the
  heating is on full blast, and it photographs beautifully.

All 9 picks under $25: https://decorly.homes/cozy-throw-blankets-renters-under-25/
