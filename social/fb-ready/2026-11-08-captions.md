## 2. Renter-friendly winter curtain ideas that keep the heat in
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-08/pin-winter-curtains.jpg
Pin title (93 chars): 10 Renter-Friendly Winter Curtain Ideas That Keep the Heat In – Thermal Curtains, Draft Fixes
Intended board (pending): Small Apartment Ideas

https://decorly.homes/renter-friendly-winter-curtain-ideas-keep-heat-in

🪟 Winter curtains are the cosiest draft fix a renter can make: heavier fabric, a better seal and a little layering can make a cold window feel warm — no drilling, no landlord sign-off.
🌿 Hang thermal curtains on a tension rod: the rod wedges between the window frame with pressure alone, so nothing is screwed in.
🌿 Add a thermal lining to curtains you already own: clip-on or hook-on linings give existing curtains a warm inner layer for a fraction of the cost of new ones.
🌿 Close them at dusk, open them at dawn: the simple habit keeps the night's cold out and lets the daytime sun warm the room for free.

## 5. How to style air plants in a small apartment this winter
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-08/pin-air-plants-winter.jpg
Pin title (88 chars): How to Style Air Plants in a Small Apartment This Winter – Air Plant Care, Winter Plants
Intended board (pending): Houseplants

https://decorly.homes/style-air-plants-small-apartment-winter-guide

🌿 Air plants are the perfect winter houseplant for a small apartment: they need no soil, no pot and barely any space — just bright light, moving air and a weekly soak. Eight steps to styling them through the cold months.
🌿 Find the brightest spot you have: air plants need more light in winter, so move them to your sunniest window.
🌿 Hang them where the air moves: near a window or doorway, in a hanging globe or on a wall mount, where air circulates and they dry properly.
🌿 Water with a weekly soak: a 20–30 minute soak once a week, then let them dry upside down so no water sits in the leaves.
