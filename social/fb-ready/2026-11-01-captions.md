## 1. Winter plant care kits under $25

🪴 Winter is the season that sorts new plant parents from confident ones — short days, dry heated air, and soil that stays wet far longer than in summer.
🌿 Overwatering is the number one winter houseplant killer, because cooler rooms and weaker light mean soil dries far more slowly — water when the soil is dry, not on a schedule, and a moisture meter tells you plainly whether the plant actually needs it.
🌿 Self-watering globes release water slowly as the soil dries, smoothing out the swings once the radiators come on.
🌿 A pebble tray raises humidity right around the plant as the water evaporates — keep the pot sitting on the pebbles, not in the water.

## 2. Christmas plants that bloom all winter
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-01/pin-christmas-plants-bloom.jpg
Pin title (92 chars): 10 Christmas Plants That Bloom All Winter (Beyond Poinsettias) – Christmas Cactus, Amaryllis

https://decorly.homes/christmas-plants-bloom-all-winter-ideas

🌺 Everyone knows the poinsettia — but it is far from the only plant that flowers in winter, and many bloom from December right through to February and beyond.
🌿 A Christmas cactus flowers because the nights get longer, so a room that is not flooded with artificial light in the evening will see it burst into flower — give it bright indirect light, water when the top inch of compost feels dry, and keep it away from radiators.
🌿 An amaryllis needs a bright spot and a cool room, or the flowers rush through and fade fast — a cool, bright room gives blooms that last for weeks, and keeping the bulb after flowering means it can bloom again next winter.
🌿 Paperwhites need no chilling period and flower within four to six weeks — a bright, cool windowsill keeps them sturdy, and staggering the planting by a fortnight gives a second wave in January.

## 3. Renter-friendly Christmas bathroom decor
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-01/pin-christmas-bathroom-decor.jpg
Pin title (97 chars): 10 Renter-Friendly Christmas Bathroom Decor Ideas – Festive Towels, Battery Candles, Fairy Lights

https://decorly.homes/renter-friendly-christmas-bathroom-decor-ideas

🛁 The bathroom is the room most people forget at Christmas — which is exactly why decorating it feels like such a treat, and why a little festive detail goes a very long way.
🌿 A festive hand towel is the single fastest Christmas swap in any bathroom: right at eye level, no fixings at all, and a plain red set simply becomes an everyday towel again in January.
🌿 A mini tree just 20 to 30 centimetres tall is plenty in a small bathroom — stand it in a pot, mug, or tin on a windowsill or shelf, with a coaster underneath so the base cannot scratch the sill.
🌿 Battery candles give all the atmosphere with none of the risk — no naked flame near towels, no wax on tiles, and a built-in timer switches them on in the evening and off before bed.
