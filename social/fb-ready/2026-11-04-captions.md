## 1. Prepare a rental for winter guests without a guest room
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-04/pin-prepare-rental-winter-guests.jpg
Pin title (91 chars): How to Prepare a Rental for Winter Guests Without a Guest Room – Hosting Tips, Sleep Setups
Intended board (pending): Small Apartment Ideas

https://decorly.homes/prepare-rental-winter-guests-guide

🛏️ No guest room? No problem — a rental can host overnight visitors beautifully if you plan the four things guests actually care about: somewhere comfortable to sleep, somewhere to put their things, a bathroom that works, and a host who is not stressed.
🌿 Solve the sleep setup first: a sofa bed is ideal, or a good air mattress with a real mattress topper — with fresh sheets, two pillows per person, and a blanket warm enough for a cold house at night.
🌿 Clear one surface and one hook per guest, using removable adhesive hooks that come down cleanly, so a guest can put their phone, book, and water glass somewhere that is not the floor.
🌿 Stock the bathroom like a mini hotel: one bath towel and one hand towel per guest, a spare toothbrush, basic toiletries — plus lip balm and moisturiser, because cold air and indoor heating dry skin fast.

## 2. Make a small apartment feel cosy all winter (without Christmas decor)
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-04/pin-cosy-small-apartment-winter.jpg
Pin title (88 chars): How to Make a Small Apartment Feel Cosy All Winter – Layered Light, Warm Textiles, Scent
Intended board (pending): Small Apartment Ideas

https://decorly.homes/cozy-small-apartment-winter-guide

🕯️ Winter in a small apartment can feel either wonderfully snug or relentlessly grey — and the difference is rarely money. It is layers: light layered over light, textiles layered over textiles, and one or two small rituals.
🌿 Layer light at three heights and switch off the overhead light: one low (candles or a small lamp), one at mid-height (a table lamp or string lights), and one high (lights on top of a bookcase) — warm white only.
🌿 Swap light cotton cushion covers for velvet, wool, or chunky knit ones, add a heavy throw folded over the sofa arm, and drape a second blanket over the back of your favourite chair.
🌿 Pick one warm scent and let it be the signature of your home all season — cinnamon and clove, cedar and pine, or vanilla and amber. A simmer pot (water, a sliced orange, cinnamon sticks and cloves) costs almost nothing and humidifies dry winter air.

## 3. Grow lights under $25 for dark winter apartments
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-04/pin-grow-lights-dark-apartments.jpg
Pin title (87 chars): 9 Grow Lights Under $25 for Dark Winter Apartments – Clip-On Lamps, Bulbs, Light Strips
Intended board (pending): Houseplants

https://decorly.homes/grow-lights-dark-winter-apartments-under-25

💡 Winter is when houseplants die, and it is rarely the cold that kills them — it is the dark. Short days, weak sun, and north-facing windows leave plants starving for light from November to February, and no amount of watering fixes a light problem.
🌿 A clip-on lamp clamps to any shelf, desk edge, or pot rim and aims light exactly where your plants are — no drilling, no installation. Many include a timer and dimmer, and a 12-hour timer setting matches a winter day well.
🌿 A grow light bulb screws into any standard lamp socket, so the lamp you already own becomes a grow light — full-spectrum bulbs give plants the blue and red wavelengths they use for growth, while looking like ordinary warm light.
🌿 An LED strip sticks to the underside of a shelf and washes the plants below in even light; run them 12 to 14 hours a day in deep winter, and use removable mounting clips rather than the adhesive backing for zero residue on a landlord's shelves.
