## 3. Keep a real Christmas tree alive all December in a small apartment
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-03/pin-keep-real-tree-alive.jpg
Pin title (90 chars): How to Keep a Real Christmas Tree Alive All December in a Small Apartment – Real Tree Care
Intended board (pending): Christmas Decor

https://decorly.homes/keep-real-christmas-tree-alive-small-apartment-guide

🌲 A real tree can stay green to New Year in a small flat — if it can drink, stays cool, and never runs dry.
🌿 Saw 2–3 centimetres off the base, straight across, before it goes in the stand: a cut tree seals its trunk within hours, and that sap layer blocks water uptake.
🌿 Keep the tree away from heat sources — a tree next to a radiator can lose its needles in a week, while the same tree across the room lasts the month.
🌿 Water daily and properly: keep the stand reservoir full every single day for the whole of December, checked each morning while the kettle boils.
