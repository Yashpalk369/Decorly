## 2. Keep houseplants alive while you travel
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-31/pin-water-houseplants-holiday.jpg
Pin title (89 chars): How to Keep Houseplants Alive While You Travel – Holiday Watering, Wick Systems, Humidity

https://decorly.homes/water-houseplants-holiday-travel-guide

🌱 Holiday plant guilt is real — but a week or two away does not have to mean crispy leaves on return.
🌿 Water deeply the day before you leave, until water runs from the drainage holes, then empty the saucer — a fully soaked root ball buys far more time than a surface splash.
🌿 Group plants together, ideally in a bathroom or kitchen, so they share humidity instead of drying out alone.
🌿 For thirsty plants or longer trips, a cotton wick from a jar of water into the soil waters slowly and steadily, with no gadgets or plumbing.

## 3. Christmas decor without buying anything
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-31/pin-christmas-no-buy-decor.jpg
Pin title (93 chars): How to Decorate a Small Apartment for Christmas Without Buying Anything – Free Greenery Ideas

https://decorly.homes/small-apartment-christmas-no-buy-decor-guide

🎄 The cosiest Christmas rooms are rarely the ones with the most new purchases — just the most thought.
🌿 Start with an audit: lay out last year's lights, ribbon, ornaments and candles first, so you do not decorate twice in a small space.
🌿 Bring the outdoors in — a few pine, fir or eucalyptus stems in a jar give scent and season for free, with nothing to store in January.
🌿 Swap textiles instead of adding objects: warm throws and darker cushion covers change the whole mood of a small room in fifteen minutes.

## 4. Child-proof Christmas decor in a rental
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-31/pin-child-proof-christmas-decor.jpg
Pin title (71 chars): How to Child-Proof Christmas Decor in a Rental – Kid-Safe Holiday Decor

https://decorly.homes/child-proof-christmas-decor-rental-guide

🎄 A calmer Christmas, not a smaller one — child-proofing the decor keeps the magic and loses the worry.
🌿 Anchor the tree without drilling a single hole, so it cannot topple.
🌿 Go shatterproof on the lower branches, where little hands reach first.
🌿 Hide every cord and battery pack, and make the fireplace and candles flameless.

## 5. Christmas bedroom decor for small apartments
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-31/pin-christmas-bedroom-decor.jpg
Pin title (76 chars): 10 Christmas Bedroom Decor Ideas for Small Apartments – Cozy Holiday Bedroom

https://decorly.homes/small-apartment-christmas-bedroom-decor-ideas/

🎄 A small room, properly festive — the bedroom gets Christmas too.
🌿 Fairy lights along the headboard are the single biggest glow for the least money.
🌿 A mini tree on the dresser and a wreath above the bed do the decorating without taking floor space.
🌿 Finish with a chunky throw and festive cushions — the whole room changes with a bedding swap.
