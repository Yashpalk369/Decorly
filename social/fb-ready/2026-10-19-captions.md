🌿 Every October the same rental plant tragedy plays out: the heating clicks on, windows start weeping condensation, and summer-thriving houseplants begin dropping yellow leaves.

3 renter-friendly fixes from the 7-step guide:
• Banish drafts — move plants 30–60 cm back from ice-cold glass (tropicals like monstera and calathea suffer first)
• Water less — learn the winter rule, and stop feeding until spring
• Fight dry heated air with pebble trays and grouping plants together

All 7 steps cost almost nothing and need zero drilling. Follow Decorly for budget, renter-friendly decor 🌱
