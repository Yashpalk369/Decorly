## 1. Keep cut Christmas greenery fresh
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-29/pin-keep-cut-greenery-fresh.jpg
Pin title (87 chars): How to Keep Cut Christmas Greenery Fresh All December – Fresh Garland Care, Wreath Tips

Full Recipe-style caption (link first):
https://decorly.homes/keep-cut-christmas-greenery-fresh-guide

🎄 Still lush on New Year's Day? Treat cut greenery like cut flowers, not plastic decor.
🌿 Buy fresh: bend a branch tip — it should flex, not snap — and shake lightly; a shower of needles means it's already half-dry.
🌿 Give it what a cut stem needs: water, cool air, and protection from heat — never hang it over a radiator.
🌿 Every step is renter-friendly and costs almost nothing, but adds days or weeks to your garland or wreath.

## 5. Pet-proof Christmas decor in a rental
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-29/pin-pet-proof-christmas-decor.jpg
Pin title (91 chars): How to Pet-Proof Christmas Decor in a Rental Without Losing Your Deposit – Pet-Safe Holiday

https://decorly.homes/pet-proof-christmas-decor-rental-guide

🐾 Cats, dogs and Christmas trees are a deposit-risk combination — unless you plan for it.
🌿 Anchor the tree without drilling a hole, so a climbing cat cannot topple it.
🌿 Hang fragile ornaments high and shatterproof ones low, where paws and tails reach.
🌿 Hide and protect every cord, skip tinsel at pet height, and keep the tree water out of reach.
