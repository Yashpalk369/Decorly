## 2. Trailing plants in a small apartment this winter

🌿 Winter is when a small apartment needs plants most — trailing vines draw the eye up, so the room feels taller, for zero floor space.
🌿 A pothos on a high shelf falls like a green curtain and turns a bookcase into a living feature — water sparingly in winter and check the soil, not the calendar.

## 3. Small apartment Christmas kitchen decor
Creative: social/pins/2026-10-30/pin-christmas-kitchen-decor.jpg
Pin title (87 chars): 10 Small Apartment Christmas Kitchen Decor Ideas – Garland, Wreaths, Fairy Lights, Mugs

https://decorly.homes/small-apartment-christmas-kitchen-decor-ideas

🎄 A small kitchen at Christmas has two jobs: feel festive and stay usable — so decorate the edges, not the middle.
🌿 Garland along the top of the cabinets adds instant Christmas without touching a centimetre of counter — and hides the dusty gap above them.
🌿 One small wreath on a cabinet door does more festive work than a shelf of ornaments, and costs nothing in counter space.
🌿 Under-cabinet fairy lights wash the counters in warm light and take up zero space — battery lights with a timer, warm white over multicolour.
