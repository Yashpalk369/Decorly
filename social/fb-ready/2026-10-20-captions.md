🪴 Small flat + Christmas = living greenery instead of another box of ornaments. Every plant in this guide costs under $20 and pulls double duty as a houseplant after the holidays.

3 picks for tiny spaces:
• Mini poinsettia pots (set of 4 for $12–16) — cluster red, cream and pink minis on a windowsill for an expensive-looking display
• Norfolk Island pine ($15–20) — the living miniature tree that doubles as your actual Christmas tree, pet-safe, grows slowly for years
• Rosemary cone topiary ($10–14) — looks like a miniature Christmas tree, smells incredible, and you can cook with it

⚠️ Pet note: several festive favourites (poinsettia, amaryllis, cyclamen) are mildly toxic to cats and dogs — the guide flags them all. Follow Decorly for budget, renter-friendly decor 🎄

Caption:
Full guide: https://decorly.homes/small-apartment-christmas-tree-ideas/

🌲 A full-size tree in a studio is a space crime — but skipping the tree feels wrong. Every idea in this guide costs $30 or less and fits a genuinely small living room.

3 format-changers:
• Pencil tree — 5–6 ft tall but only 60 cm wide at the base ($20–25), full tree presence in a corner that would hold a floor lamp
• Flat-back half tree — full-looking from the front, half the depth against the wall ($25–30), stores flat against a wardrobe
• 3-foot tabletop mini in a basket — zero floor footprint, and the basket hides ornaments inside for January storage

All 10 pack away small in January. Follow Decorly for budget, renter-friendly decor ✨

## 3. pin-living-room-storage-hacks.jpg
Post: 14 Small Living Room Storage Hacks That Don't Look Like Storage
Link: https://decorly.homes/small-living-room-storage-hacks/

Caption:
Full guide: https://decorly.homes/small-living-room-storage-hacks/

📦 Small living rooms have a storage paradox: you need MORE storage than a big room, but have LESS space for it. The fix isn't more shelves — it's storage that disappears into the room.

3 stealth hacks:
• Storage ottoman instead of a coffee table — seating, footrest, and hidden storage in one
• Console table behind the sofa — turns dead space into a stash zone
• One tall narrow bookcase instead of a wide one — uses wall height without eating floor space

14 hacks total, all renter-friendly, plus the free "one-in-one-out" rule. Follow Decorly for budget, renter-friendly decor 🏠
