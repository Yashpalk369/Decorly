## 1. Cosy November decor for small apartments
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-05/pin-cosy-november-decor.jpg
Pin title (93 chars): 10 Cosy November Decor Ideas for Small Apartments – Autumn Apartment Decor, Budget Fall Ideas
Intended board (pending): Fall & Thanksgiving

https://decorly.homes/cozy-november-decor-small-apartments-ideas

🍂 November is the awkward month for small apartments — the pumpkins have gone soft, the Christmas boxes feel premature, and the flat sits in a grey in-between. The fix is not more stuff, it is a handful of warm, autumn-into-winter touches that make the flat feel finished right now, without a single Christmas bauble.
🌿 Swap in one chunky knit throw: folded over the sofa arm, it is the single highest-impact swap in a small flat — texture reads as temperature, and a heavy knit instantly makes a light summer sofa look winter-ready. Choose one warm neutral (cream, oatmeal or rust) so it works with everything you own.
🌿 Build a candlelight evening ritual: switch the ceiling light off and light three pillar candles of different heights on a tray on the coffee table. The tray corrals the candles into one deliberate display and protects the surface underneath.
🌿 Hang a dried orange garland: slice oranges thin, dry them low in the oven, and thread them on twine with cinnamon sticks and rosemary. It looks autumnal now, festive in December, and costs almost nothing.

## 2. How to dry citrus and flowers for winter decor
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-05/pin-dry-citrus-flowers.jpg
Pin title (93 chars): How to Dry Citrus and Flowers for Winter Decor – Dried Orange Slices, Natural Christmas Decor
Intended board (pending): Christmas Decor

https://decorly.homes/dry-citrus-flowers-winter-decor-guide

🍊 Dried citrus and flowers are the hardest-working winter decor there is: they cost almost nothing, they last for months, they smell wonderful, and they suit every style from rustic to minimal. A garland of dried orange slices glows like stained glass in a window.
🌿 Slice citrus thin and evenly — about 5 millimetres thick, roughly the width of a pencil. Uneven slices dry unevenly: thin edges burn while thick centres stay wet.
🌿 Oven-dry citrus low and slow: lay the slices in a single layer on a wire rack over a baking tray — the rack is essential, because slices dried flat on a tray steam on the underside and stick. It takes about three hours of mostly unattended time.
🌿 Air-dry flowers upside down in small bunches, hung in a warm, dark, dry room. Darkness preserves colour — light bleaches dried flowers to straw within days — and small bunches dry faster because air circulates between the stems.

## 3. Eucalyptus decor ideas for winter
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-05/pin-eucalyptus-decor.jpg
Pin title (84 chars): 10 Eucalyptus Decor Ideas for Winter – Fresh Eucalyptus, Winter Greenery Decor Ideas
Intended board (pending): Christmas Decor

https://decorly.homes/eucalyptus-decor-winter-ideas

🌿 Eucalyptus is winter's easiest luxury. A bunch costs a few pounds or dollars, it lasts for weeks fresh and months dried, it smells clean and calm without a candle in sight, and its soft blue-green colour flatters every interior from white minimal to dark maximal.
🌿 Hang a eucalyptus bundle in the shower: tie a bunch of fresh eucalyptus to the shower head or a hook, away from the direct water stream, and the steam releases its scent every time you shower — a spa ritual that costs less than a bottle of shower gel.
🌿 Fill a tall vase with long stems and nothing else — no filler flowers, no arranging skill, no colour matching. The stems' natural arch and soft colour do all the work.
🌿 Make a eucalyptus wreath for the door: wire small bundles of eucalyptus around a wire or grapevine base in one direction, overlapping each bundle over the last. Soft, green, fragrant, and right from November through February.

## 4. Protect rental floors from winter salt, snow and mud
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-05/pin-protect-rental-floors.jpg
Pin title (90 chars): How to Protect Rental Floors from Winter Salt, Snow and Mud – Winter Floor Protection Tips
Intended board (pending): Small Apartment Ideas

https://decorly.homes/protect-rental-floors-winter-salt-guide

🧂 Winter is the season that eats rental deposits. Every time someone walks in with snowy boots, they track in water, grit and road salt — and salt is the quiet villain, because it does not just dirty a floor, it damages it: salt crystals scratch wood, stain grout, eat into laminate seams and leave white rings on stone.
🌿 Start with a boot tray at the door: choose a tray with a raised lip and a ridged or slatted base — the lip holds meltwater, the ridges keep boots out of the puddle — and size it to the number of people in the household.
🌿 Lay a washable runner from the door toward the living space: it catches the second wave — the drips, the grit shaken loose mid-stride, the salt that rode in on a coat hem.
🌿 Make shoe removal easy, not a lecture: a small bench or sturdy stool by the entrance, a basket or open shelf for shoes, and a pair of indoor slippers waiting in plain sight turn shoe removal from a rule into the path of least resistance.

## 5. Winter candle holders under $25 for small apartments
Creative: social/pins/2026-11-05/pin-winter-candle-holders.jpg
Pin title (89 chars): 9 Winter Candle Holders Under $25 for Small Apartments – Cosy Candle Decor, Budget Winter
Intended board (pending): Budget Decor Finds

https://decorly.homes/winter-candle-holders-small-apartments-under-25

🕯️ Candlelight is the cheapest warmth a small apartment can buy, but a bare candle on a bare table looks like an afterthought. A candle holder turns the same candle into a deliberate display: it catches the wax, frames the flame, and gives the light a place to belong. Every holder below is under $25.
🌿 Black metal lantern: a lantern frames a candle in glass and metal, which makes the flame look intentional and keeps it safe from draughts, curtains and curious pets. The glass panels magnify the glow, so one candle lights more of the room than a bare pillar ever would.
🌿 Brass taper holders, set of two: brass taper holders lift candlelight to eye level, where it flatters a room most, and brass itself catches and throws the light around in a way no other material does.
🌿 Wooden pillar candle tray: a wooden tray corrals several pillar candles into one display, protects the surface underneath from wax and heat, and adds a warm natural texture that suits winter perfectly. Three pillars of different heights on one tray is the classic stylist arrangement.
