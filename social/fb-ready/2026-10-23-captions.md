🎁 A living plant is the rare gift that gets better after the wrapping comes off — and every pick here is under $20, gift-ready or needs only a ribbon. From first-time plant parents to the friend whose flat is already a jungle.

🌸 Tip 1: Phalaenopsis orchid gift set ($15–20) — the tuxedo of plant gifts. Looks like you spent three times the price, blooms for 2–3 months, and reblooms yearly on an ice cube's worth of water a week.
💚 Tip 2: Hoya heart ($8–12) — a single heart-shaped leaf in a tiny terracotta pot, the sweetest $10 in the plant world. It's a succulent, so it thrives on neglect: water every couple of weeks.
🌺 Tip 3: Amaryllis bulb gift box ($10–15) — the recipient plants it and watches it explode into huge trumpet flowers within 6–8 weeks. A gift AND an activity. Buy early November for Christmas blooms.
