9 Peel-and-Stick Christmas Decor Finds Under $20 for Renters

Rental-Friendly Decor October 27, 2026·2,116 words

9 peel-and-stick Christmas decor finds under $20 for renters — window clings, wall decals, garlands and more that peel off clean in January.

Peel-and-stick Christmas decor is the renter's best friend, because it delivers the two things rentals usually forbid: big visual impact and zero evidence in January. No holes, no nails, no adhesive residue if you buy the right kind — just press it on, enjoy it for six weeks, and peel it off. The catch is quality: cheap stickers curl, fade and leave ghosts on the paint, while good ones look like they were painted on and come off like they were never there.

Every find below is chosen for clean removal on rental surfaces, a price under $20, and a look that reads as decoration rather than a child's sticker sheet. The golden rule runs through all of them: stick them to smooth, clean surfaces, never to fresh paint, and peel slowly in January when the room is warm — cold adhesive is the adhesive that fights back.

1. Snowflake window clings

Why it works: window clings are static, not adhesive, which means they stick to glass with no glue at all and peel off leaving literally nothing. A sheet of snowflakes turns a plain rental window into a Christmas card view, visible from inside and from the street, for the price of a coffee.

Where it fits: on any window, mirror or glass door, scattered naturally rather than in a perfect grid — real snow does not fall in rows. Press out air bubbles with a credit card, and if a cling loses its grip mid-December, a quick wipe with a damp cloth restores the static. Store them flat between sheets of baking paper and they last for years. Shop snowflake window clings

2. A peel-and-stick Christmas tree wall decal

Why it works: a wall-decal Christmas tree is the answer for renters with no room — or no permission — for a real tree. It gives you the shape, the ornaments and the focal point of a tree on a flat wall, and it peels off without taking paint with it, provided the paint is fully cured and the decal is made for walls rather than for laptops.

Where it fits: on the largest empty wall in the living room, at a height where the "tree" reads as a tree — base near the floor, top at eye level or above. Add a few real ornaments hung from removable hooks at the decal's edges and the illusion is surprisingly convincing, especially in the evening with fairy lights draped around it. Keep it off textured wallpaper, where removal can lift the texture. Shop peel-and-stick Christmas tree decals

3. Removable fairy-light wall decals

Why it works: a string of fairy-light decals gives you the glow of lights on a wall where real lights would need hooks, cables and a socket — and it works in daylight too, when real lights are invisible. The best versions have a slight shimmer or metallic finish that catches the light like a real bulb.

Where it fits: draped across a wall above a sofa, around a doorway, or framing a mirror. Pair them with one real string of lights elsewhere in the room so the eye reads "lights" rather than "stickers", and keep the decal line slightly sagging between points rather than ruler-straight — a gentle swag looks like a real light string, a straight line looks like a diagram. Shop fairy light wall decals

4. Peel-and-stick garland for doorframes

Why it works: a garland transforms a doorway, and a peel-and-stick version does it without a single hook. The adhesive-backed pine garlands now on the market look convincingly full from a normal viewing distance, and they conform to doorframes, mirror edges and stair rails that hooks cannot follow.

Where it fits: around the main living-room doorway, where everyone passes under it, or along the top of a large mirror. Press it firmly along its whole length, not just at the ends, or the middle will sag by week two. In January, peel from one end slowly, warming any stubborn section with a hairdryer for ten seconds — heat is the secret weapon for clean removal of every adhesive on this list. Shop peel-and-stick Christmas garland

5. Christmas village window stickers

Why it works: a village silhouette across the bottom of a window is the most charming window decoration there is, and the static-cling versions cost under $10, need no tools and leave no residue. At dusk, with the room lights on behind them, the little houses glow like a real street.

Where it fits: along the bottom edge of a living-room window, or across a glass balcony door. Layer them: village at the bottom, snowflakes above, and a string of real fairy lights behind the glass, and the window becomes the room's main decoration. Because they are static clings, you can reposition them as often as you like — try the arrangement, step back, move a house, repeat. Shop Christmas village window stickers

6. Removable snow spray stencils

Why it works: snow spray with a stencil gives you the frosted-window look of a Christmas film set, and it wipes off glass completely with a damp cloth. It is the only decoration on this list that is genuinely free-form — you choose the pattern, the density and the placement, and a mistake wipes away in seconds.

Where it fits: on windows you do not need to see through clearly for six weeks — a hallway window, a bathroom window, or the corners of a living-room window. Use a stencil for crisp shapes (snowflakes, stars, a village skyline) rather than spraying freehand, which tends to look like an accident. Spray lightly in two coats rather than one heavy coat, and keep it off window frames and sills, where it can be harder to clean. Shop snow spray window stencils

7. Peel-and-stick stocking holders for the wall

Why it works: stockings need a hook, and rentals without mantels need a wall solution. Adhesive stocking hooks designed for the job hold a filled stocking's weight, stick to smooth walls and tile, and remove cleanly — solving the no-mantel problem without a single screw. They also let you hang stockings somewhere visible, rather than draped over a radiator.

Where it fits: in a row on a hallway wall, on the side of a bookshelf, or on a tiled kitchen wall near the festivities. Hang them at a height where stockings clear the floor even when stuffed, and do not overload them — a stocking full of oranges and chocolate is heavier than an empty one, and the hook's rating assumes the full weight. For the full no-mantel guide, see our stocking-holder roundup for apartments. Shop adhesive stocking hooks

8. Washi-tape Christmas trees and bunting

Why it works: washi tape is the crafter's peel-and-stick — it comes in every Christmas pattern, sticks to walls and doors, and peels off without a mark, because it was designed to be repositioned. A washi-tape tree, a washi bunting string or a washi frame around a mirror costs a few pounds and looks deliberately handmade rather than cheap.

Where it fits: anywhere you want a quick, playful decoration — a hallway, a child's bedroom wall, the frame of a mirror, or the edge of a bookshelf. The trick is confidence: lay the tape in bold, simple shapes (a big triangle tree, a long bunting swag) rather than fiddly detail, and step back often to check the overall shape. Washi tape is also the best decoration on this list for involving children, because a misplaced strip peels off and tries again. Shop Christmas washi tape

9. Peel-and-stick tile decals for the kitchen

Why it works: the kitchen is where Christmas cooking happens and where decoration is usually forgotten, because tiles and splashbacks take no hooks. Peel-and-stick tile decals — little holly sprigs, stars or gingerbread shapes — dress the backsplash for December and peel off in January, and they are made to survive kitchen heat and steam.

Where it fits: scattered across a tiled backsplash, on the front of a plain cabinet door, or framing the kitchen window. Keep them away from direct hob heat, which can bake adhesive on, and choose decals labelled removable rather than permanent — the two look identical in the packet and behave very differently in January. A small sheet of decals goes a long way: ten or twelve sprigs, well placed, beat a wall covered edge to edge. Shop Christmas tile decals
