About Decorly

Beautiful homes on a renter's budget

Decorly started with a simple frustration: most home decor advice assumes you own the place. Knock down that wall! Refinish those floors! Install custom built-ins! Great — unless you're renting, in which case your "renovation budget" is zero and your landlord has opinions.

We write for the rest of us: renters, apartment dwellers, and anyone decorating a small space on a real budget. Every idea we publish is reversible, affordable, and tested against one question — can you do this without losing your deposit?

What we cover

  • Small space decor — layouts, storage, and styling tricks that make tiny rooms feel bigger
  • Rental-friendly upgrades — peel-and-stick, no-drill, and temporary fixes that actually look good
  • Plants & greenery — biophilic decor for beginners, including dark apartments with bad light
  • 2026 trends — Afrohemian, Neo Deco, FunHaus maximalism, and celestial accents, translated for real budgets

How we make money (the honest version)

Decorly is reader-supported. When you buy through links on our pages, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you — that's how we keep the lights on. We only recommend products we'd buy ourselves, and affiliate relationships never decide what we cover. Sponsored content, if we ever run it, will always be labeled.

Questions, corrections, or a rental win to share? Reach us at hello@decorly.co.
