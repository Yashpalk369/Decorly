9 Holiday Plant Gifts Under $20

Plants & Greenery October 23, 2026·1,691 words

9 holiday plant gifts under $20 — orchids, hoya hearts, grow lights and gift-ready plant sets for the plant lover in your life.

A living plant is the rare gift that gets better after the wrapping comes off — it grows, it blooms, and it reminds the recipient of you every time they water it. And unlike most Christmas gifts, a good plant gift costs less than a takeaway dinner. Every pick below is under $20, ships gift-ready or needs only a ribbon, and suits everyone from first-time plant parents to the friend whose flat is already a jungle.

Shopping for your own festive greenery too? Our Christmas plants for small apartments covers the best ones to keep.

1. Phalaenopsis orchid gift set

The orchid is the tuxedo of plant gifts: a white phalaenopsis in a simple ceramic pot ($15–20 at supermarkets from late November) looks like you spent three times the price. Blooms last two to three months, and the plant reblooms yearly with minimal care — bright indirect light and an ice cube's worth of water a week is the whole routine.

Why it works: it arrives looking like a florist arrangement but lives like a houseplant. Nobody has ever been disappointed by an orchid.

Where it fits: the universal safe gift — mother-in-law, host, colleague, neighbour. Shop orchid gift sets

2. Monstera deliciosa (4-inch pot)

For the trendy friend: a young monstera ($12–16) with its first split leaves is the most recognisable "cool plant" in existence, and a 4-inch pot is the perfect starter size — impressive now, with years of dramatic growth ahead. Tie a ribbon around the pot and it's done; no wrapping paper survives a plant anyway.

Why it works: monsteras are famously forgiving — bright indirect light, water when dry — so even a nervous beginner keeps it alive. It's the rare trendy gift that's also practical.

Where it fits: friends under 35, new-flat housewarmings, the colleague with the aesthetic desk. Shop monstera deliciosa

3. Hoya heart (Hoya kerrii)

A single heart-shaped leaf in a tiny terracotta pot ($8–12) is the sweetest $10 in the plant world — it literally looks like a green heart. It's a succulent, so it thrives on neglect: water every couple of weeks and bright light. Fair warning: the single-leaf version rarely grows further, but nobody receiving one cares.

Why it works: it's a Valentine's-level romantic gesture at a stocking-filler price, and it fits on the smallest desk or sill. The novelty factor is off the charts.

Where it fits: partners, best friends, Secret Santa with a $15 limit. Shop hoya heart plants

4. Pilea peperomioides

The "friendship plant" or Chinese money plant ($12–18) earns its nickname: it constantly produces baby offshoots the recipient can pot up and give away, turning one gift into many. The round coin leaves are sculptural and photogenic, and care is easy — bright indirect light, water when the top inch dries.

Why it works: the symbolism does the heavy lifting. Giving a plant that multiplies into gifts for others is a genuinely lovely gesture, and pileas are nearly indestructible.

Where it fits: close friends, siblings, anyone starting their plant collection. Shop pilea peperomioides

5. Air plant Christmas ornament set

Glass baubles with living air plants inside ($12–18 for a set of 4–6) hang straight on the recipient's tree — decor and gift in one. Air plants need no soil and almost no care: a misting or a 20-minute soak every week or two. After Christmas the baubles move to a windowsill and keep going.

Why it works: it's two gifts disguised as one, and it solves the "what do I get the person who has everything" problem. Zero potting, zero mess, zero skill required.

Where it fits: tree owners, minimalists, the hard-to-buy-for relative. Shop air plant ornaments

6. Full-spectrum grow light bulb

The practical gift plant people never buy themselves: a full-spectrum LED bulb ($12–18) that screws into any standard lamp and keeps houseplants thriving through dark winter months. It's the difference between a plant collection that survives January and one that sulks until April. Pair it with our winter plant care guide as a card insert.

Why it works: every plant lover with north-facing windows secretly wants one. It's useful from the day it's unwrapped, and it makes all their other plants better.

Where it fits: serious plant collectors, anyone in a dark flat — see our low-light plant picks for what to grow under it. Shop grow light bulbs

7. Ceramic planter trio gift set

Three small ceramic planters in coordinated colours ($14–20 for the set) are the gift that upgrades every plant the recipient already owns. Most plant lovers have healthy plants sitting in ugly nursery pots — this fixes that in one box. Look for sets with drainage holes and saucers; without drainage, they're just decoration.

Why it works: it's the rare plant gift that suits experts and beginners equally, and it can't die. Even the most plant-saturated friend has a nursery pot that needs replacing.

Where it fits: the friend with "too many plants" (there's no such thing, but their shelves say otherwise). Shop ceramic planter sets

8. Glass mister + pruning shears gift set

A beautiful glass mister and a pair of sharp micro pruning shears ($12–18 as a set) turn plant care from a chore into a ritual. The mister handles humidity-loving plants and the shears make deadheading and propagating clean and easy — two tools every plant owner reaches for weekly. Wrap with a sprig of eucalyptus and it looks boutique.

Why it works: tools are the most-gifted-least-bought category in every hobby. It says "I take your plant thing seriously" without needing to know a single Latin name.

Where it fits: the established plant parent, the propagation enthusiast. Shop plant care gift sets

9. Amaryllis bulb gift box

The classic Christmas plant gift: a boxed amaryllis kit ($10–15) that the recipient plants and watches explode into huge trumpet flowers within 6–8 weeks. It's interactive — genuinely thrilling to watch — and the boxed kit format means it ships perfectly and needs no care until opened. Buy early November for Christmas blooms.

Why it works: it's a gift and an activity. Few presents under $15 deliver six weeks of anticipation followed by flowers the size of your hand.

Where it fits: grandparents, hosts, anyone who enjoys a project. Shop amaryllis gift kits
